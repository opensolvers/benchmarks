# collideSoA objdump evidence — vector_size RVV vs prior POD RVV
# date=2026-08-02  host=orangepirv2  g++ 14.3 -O2 -march=rv64gcv

## Hot-function instruction counts

| backend | vle64 | vse64 | vfadd | vfmul | vfmacc+vfmadd | vfsqrt | mem | vf* | vf*/mem |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| POD RVV (double[4] spill) | 71 | 61 | 11 | 17 | ~0 | 1 | 132 | 29 | 0.22 |
| vector_size RVV (f64x4_t) | 48 | 18 | 22 | 20 | 14 | 2 | 66 | 58 | 0.88 |
| FORCE_SCALAR (auto-vec) | 38 | 27 | 8 | 20 | 14 | 2 | 65 | 44 | 0.68 |

## Takeaway
vector_size cuts hot vse roughly 3× and mem ops ~2× vs POD, while arithmetic
density rises (including FMAs). Still not faster than FORCE_SCALAR on this SoA
microbench (WALL 14.60s vs 8.34s).
